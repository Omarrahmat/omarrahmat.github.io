27 October 2026Northern IrelandResearch

Children in care in Northern Ireland reach record high, official figures show

Department of Health statistics show 4,287 children were looked after in Northern Ireland at 31 March 2026, the highest since 1995, with 83 per cent living in foster care and more carers leaving than being approved.

27 October 2026PolicyRecruitment

Government launches England's first national foster carer recruitment drive

The Department for Education has launched a nationally coordinated fostering recruitment campaign from Downing Street, aimed at people in their 30s and 40s, as part of its plan for 10,000 more foster places.

27 October 2026RecruitmentCouncils

Eleven projects win Fostering Innovation Fund backing to trial new ways of fostering

Regional recruitment hubs and care cooperatives will use a share of £12.4m to test weekend fostering, specialist remand placements, parent-and-child fostering and better support during allegations.
