Cookie notice

Last updated 28 October 2026

The short version

Fostering Today does not use advertising, tracking or profiling cookies, so there is no cookie banner to accept.

What runs on this site

  • Analytics. We use Plausible Analytics, which works without cookies and without storing anything on your device. It counts visits in aggregate and does not follow you around the web.
  • Fonts. The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Google receives your IP address and browser details to deliver them. This does not set a tracking cookie on this site.
  • Paying us. If you subscribe to a placement you leave this site for a Stripe checkout page. Stripe uses its own cookies there, for security and fraud prevention. Their policy is at stripe.com/cookies-policy/legal.
  • Forms. When you send a form, it goes to our database. Nothing is kept on your device.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site works the same without them. See our privacy notice for how we use personal data, or email hello@fosteringtoday.com with any question.
