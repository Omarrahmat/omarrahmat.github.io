Fostering in Kent

Children in care in Kent are looked after by Kent County Council's own fostering service, Kent Fostering, and by the independent fostering agencies listed on this page. The council says the number of children in its care is rising and that it wants children to find safe homes within their own communities, so that they can stay close to school, friends and family. It looks for carers from every community, whatever their ethnicity, sexual orientation, disability, age or marital status, and no formal qualifications are needed.

Kent is also one of 20 councils in Local Authority Fostering South East, a regional partnership funded by the Department for Education. When it launched, the partnership said there were more than 11,000 children in care across the South East but fewer than 3,000 foster carers approved by councils. The hub gives prospective carers one place to make a first enquiry and the chance to talk to an experienced foster carer before deciding.

Kent sets its rates each year. For 2026-27 the weekly maintenance allowance for each child runs from £210 for a child under two to £320.92 for a young person over 16. Carers looking after a child who is not related to them also receive a weekly reward payment of £156.70 for a child aged up to 8 or £297.64 for a child aged 9 to 18, with extra skills and disability payments where they apply. Our Kent allowance page has the full breakdown.

Kent Fostering says its carers get round-the-clock support, local training and a route to progress. Agencies set their own fees and support, so it is worth speaking to the council and two or three of the agencies below, and asking each about training, out-of-hours help and what they pay, before you choose.

Sources: Urgent call for foster carers (Kent County Council news); Kent County Council's fostering service joins forces with other South East local authorities to recruit more foster carers; Kent County Council Record of Decision 26/00004: rates for children's social care services 2026-27; Kent foster carer allowance 2026-27 (Fostering Today).
