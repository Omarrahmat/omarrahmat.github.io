Fostering in Knowsley

People in Knowsley can foster with the council's own service, Foster for Knowsley, or with one of the independent agencies listed above. The council takes enquiries through the Foster4 website and phone line, and its own pages set out plainly who can apply.

You do not need to own your home, be married, drive or hold qualifications, and there is no upper age limit; you do need a spare bedroom and some experience of being around young people, whether through your own family or your work. Single people are welcome, sexual orientation plays no part in the assessment, and a disability is not a bar in itself, although every applicant has a medical. Working carers can sometimes foster school-age or older children, while younger children or those with additional needs may need someone at home all day. The council also runs a support group for men who foster.

Becoming a carer starts with an enquiry and a home visit from an experienced social worker, followed by the Skills to Foster course, where you meet local carers and young people with experience of care, and then a full Form F assessment. The council says the whole process can take up to eight months from the first enquiry. Approved carers get an allowance for each child, a skills payment once they gain qualifications, a dedicated social worker, an out-of-hours support line, free training and equipment such as cots and stair gates.

Knowsley also recruits specialist foster carers for children with complex emotional, behavioural or psychological needs, often people with backgrounds in health, education, social care or the emergency services. It offers this role a professional fee of up to £1,000 a week, with more intensive training and regular supervision. Speak to the council and two or three of the services above, and see the Liverpool and St Helens pages if you live near the borough's edge.

Sources: Becoming a foster carer (Foster for Knowsley); Our support for carers (Foster for Knowsley); Fostering FAQ (Foster for Knowsley); Specialist foster carers (Knowsley Council).
