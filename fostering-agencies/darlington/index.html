Fostering in Darlington

Children in care in Darlington are looked after by Darlington Borough Council's own fostering service and by the independent agencies listed on this page. The council describes its fostering team as small, friendly and not-for-profit, and says no special qualifications are needed to apply.

In its May 2025 appeal the council said it urgently needed carers for children of all ages, for both short and long-term care, and particularly people who can keep brothers and sisters together, care for teenagers, or look after children with complex needs and disabilities. Its applications are made through Foster with North East, the regional fostering partnership.

Darlington took up the Mockingbird model in November 2022, when its first group was formed. A Mockingbird group links several foster families around an experienced hub carer, who offers regular get-togethers, advice and mentoring, and planned and emergency short breaks, so carers and children have a wider family to lean on and brothers and sisters can stay in touch.

The council's fostering statement of purpose (2017) describes the range of fostering it arranges, from emergency and short-term care to short breaks, long-term placements and staying put after 18, with a supervising social worker for every carer and access to therapeutic support for children with complex needs. Ask each service what it pays and what support it gives, and speak to the council and two or three of the agencies below before choosing.

Sources: We urgently need foster carers (Darlington Borough Council, May 2025); Mockingbird (Darlington Borough Council); Statement of purpose: fostering (Darlington Borough Council).
