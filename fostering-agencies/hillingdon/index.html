Fostering in Hillingdon

In Hillingdon, the council's own fostering team works alongside Foster with West London, which handles enquiries, recruitment and assessment for the borough. Foster with West London is a regional fostering service shared by eight councils: Brent, Ealing, Hammersmith and Fulham, Harrow, Hillingdon, Hounslow, Kensington and Chelsea, and Westminster. Its stated aim is for more children in the councils' care to live in west London with well-supported foster carers. Hillingdon's fostering page notes that the council is rated Outstanding by Ofsted and says carers from all walks of life are needed as more children come into care each year.

There is no maximum age to foster with Hillingdon, but you need to be fit and healthy and have relevant experience of looking after children. Single people, LGBT+ people and disabled people are welcome to apply, and you can rent if your landlord agrees, though the council cannot accept applicants who have lodgers. Each child needs their own bedroom; some brothers and sisters may share, and a baby up to two can sleep in a cot in your room. You can work while fostering if you have flexibility around school hours and holidays, but carers for babies are expected to be at home full time. A good level of spoken and written English is required.

Applicants attend a three-day Skills to Foster preparation course as part of the assessment. Once approved, you receive a welcome pack and a supervising social worker, and the council says a first child may arrive within a few days or weeks. Hillingdon pays a weekly allowance for each child and a fee for your work, which it describes as higher than the national minimum, and it notes that foster carers get a tax relief of their own.

Speak to Foster with West London and to two or three of the agencies shown above, and compare the training, support and pay each offers.

Sources: Fostering (Hillingdon Council); Fostering myths and questions (Hillingdon Council); The fostering process: what happens next (Hillingdon Council); Contact us about fostering (Hillingdon Council); Partners (Foster with West London).
