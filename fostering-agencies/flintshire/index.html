Fostering in Flintshire

Flintshire's council fostering service is Foster Wales Flintshire, based at the county offices on Chapel Street in Flint. It is the council's own not-for-profit service and one of the 22 council teams in the Foster Wales network. Children across the county are looked after by its carers and by the services shown above.

The council says there are children of every age in Flintshire who need a family, and that it wants children to stay in their own communities and keep their cultural backgrounds. Fostering can last a few nights, a few weeks or much longer. It also looks for Next Steps carers, who help a child or young person move from a children's home into family life, and carers who work full time can offer short breaks instead. Flintshire runs the Mockingbird model, in which a group of fostering households supports one another like an extended family, and it has done so for six years.

Foster Wales Flintshire says there is no upper age limit. You can rent or own your home, be single or married, be in work or not, and your ethnicity and sexual orientation make no difference. Pets are considered as part of the assessment, and if you smoke you will not be able to foster younger children but can still foster. You will need a spare room. The first step is an enquiry or an information pack, followed if you want by an informal call.

The council has not published current rates for Flintshire carers that we could check, so ask each service for its allowance, fee and extras in writing. Our North Wales, Wrexham and Conwy pages cover nearby areas; talk to Foster Wales Flintshire and one or two of the services above before you decide.
