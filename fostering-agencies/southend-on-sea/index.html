Fostering in Southend-on-Sea

Southend-on-Sea City Council runs its own fostering service, Southend Fostering, alongside the independent agencies shown above. In May 2026 the council said it had 76 foster carers looking after 92 children and needed at least four new carers that year. It has asked in particular for carers for older children, brothers and sisters who need to stay together, disabled children and unaccompanied asylum-seeking children, and for carers who can support a mother or father and their baby living in the carer's home.

You need to be over 21 and have a spare room. The council says there is no upper age limit and welcomes people regardless of ethnic background, employment status, marital status or sexual orientation; single parents already foster with the service. The first step is a conversation with the fostering team or an information session, including virtual sessions run through the Southend Fostering website.

Southend uses the Mockingbird model, in which an experienced hub carer supports a small group of nearby fostering families with advice, check-ins, help with school runs and childcare, and get-togethers such as sibling contact at the hub home. Carers also have a dedicated social worker, access to senior mental health practitioners linked to the service and peer support groups, and the council's Edge of Care service can support the whole foster family when needed. In 2024 the council introduced activity discounts at Southend Leisure and Tennis Centre for fostering families, children in care and care leavers.

The council pays a fostering fee and an allowance for each child in placement, but its news pages do not give the figures, so ask for the current rates. Compare the council with two or three of the agencies above before you decide.

Sources: Open heart, open home: one carer's 11-year fostering journey in Southend (Southend-on-Sea City Council, 15 May 2026); Foster carers share positive benefits of fostering (Southend-on-Sea City Council); Southend foster carers shine a light on their rewarding careers (Southend-on-Sea City Council); Prospective foster carer open weekend welcomes future carers (Southend-on-Sea City Council, 30 September 2024); We're proud to support Foster Care Fortnight 2024 (Southend-on-Sea City Council, 13 May 2024).
