Fostering in Preston

Children in care from Preston are looked after by Lancashire County Council, whose fostering recruitment team works from County Hall in the city, and by independent agencies and charities such as the services shown above. The council fosters children from birth to 18 and says it always needs carers for older children and for larger groups of brothers and sisters, so that siblings can stay together. Its Foster Care Fortnight 2026 appeal named siblings and teenagers again, and said carers are urgently needed.

Preston matters in the county's own figures. Lancashire's agency foster care market position statement of April 2025 names Preston as one of four districts, with West Lancashire, Lancaster and Burnley, where requests for hard-to-find foster homes were highest; together those four made up 69% of the council's requests to agencies in 2024/25. More than half of those requests were for children aged 11 and over, and the council says it wants more agency carers living in Lancashire to care for Lancashire children, close to home.

To foster with the council you need to be over 21, have a spare bedroom for the child and have lived in the UK for the last six months. You can be single or married, rent or own your home, and work or be at home full-time; smokers can care for children aged five and over. The council describes a four to six month assessment and a three-day Skills to Foster course at County Hall in Preston. It pays weekly allowances plus welcome, holiday, birthday and festival payments, with enhanced packages for parent and child care, disabled children and sibling groups; ask the council for its current figures.

Support from the council includes your own social worker, a 24/7 advice line, a mentor who is an experienced carer, support groups and a wellbeing programme, and it says Ofsted rates its children's services Good. Fostering with the council means caring for Lancashire's own children; an agency approves, trains and supports you itself and takes children referred by councils, Lancashire among them. Talk to the council and two or three of the services above before you choose. Nearby, see our pages for Lancashire, Chorley, Blackpool and Blackburn with Darwen.

Sources: Agency Foster Care Market Position Statement, April 2025 (Lancashire County Council); Fostering information pack (Lancashire County Council); Foster Care Fortnight 2026 (Lancashire County Council); Fostering Statement of Purpose 2023-26 (Lancashire County Council); Fostering Today: fostering agencies in Lancashire; Fostering Today: fostering agencies in Chorley; Fostering Today: fostering agencies in Blackpool; Fostering Today: fostering agencies in Blackburn with Darwen.
