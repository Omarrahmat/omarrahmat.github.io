Fostering in Rochdale

In Rochdale, children in care are looked after by foster carers approved by Rochdale Borough Council's own fostering team, which takes part in the Greater Manchester fostering partnership FosterForGM, and by the independent fostering agencies listed on this page. Whichever route you choose, you will be caring for a local child or young person who needs a safe and settled home.

The council is especially keen to hear from people who could keep brothers and sisters together, which usually means having more than one spare bedroom. It also needs carers for older children and teenagers who are working towards living independently, and for disabled children from birth to 18, where carers are backed by the Children with Disability Team and health colleagues. Its Our Place scheme supports young people aged 9 and over.

New carers begin with the Skills to Foster course and then have regular training. You keep your assessing social worker for at least your first year, with social work support throughout your fostering career, and you can be paired with an experienced carer as a buddy. There are carer support groups around the borough, counselling through an employee assistance programme, input from child psychologists and a specialist nurse, and a virtual headteacher who oversees the education of children in care.

The council publishes its weekly rates. The maintenance allowance runs from £182 for a child aged 0 to 4 up to £267 for a young person aged 16 or over, and carers also receive a weekly fee of £176.16 at Level 2 or £330.31 at Level 3, with extra payments for birthdays, festivals and holidays. The council aims to complete the process within six months, and can sometimes fast-track it to four. A good next step is to speak to the council and to two or three of the agencies below, and compare their training, support and pay.

Sources: Foster carer payments and allowances (Rochdale Borough Council); Children who need fostering (Rochdale Borough Council); Support and training for foster carers (Rochdale Borough Council); How to become a foster carer (Rochdale Borough Council); Fostering (Rochdale Borough Council).
