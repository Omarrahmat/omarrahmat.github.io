Fostering in Stoke-on-Trent

Children in care in Stoke-on-Trent are looked after by carers approved by the city council's service, Fostering Stoke, or by independent fostering agencies such as those listed on this page. The council says some of its children currently live a long way from their families and that it wants to bring more of them back to the city. It recruits for long-term and short-term care, emergency care for children who come into care late at night or at weekends, and respite and weekend-only fostering.

In July 2025 the council launched a drive for Family Pathways carers, who offer a full-time home to a teenager moving on from a residential children's home. These carers receive a weekly professional fee and an age-based allowance for the young person, 20 days of paid holiday, a dedicated keyworker and peer support groups.

In June 2025 the council set out a promise of support for all its foster carers. It includes mentoring and buddying, local support groups, free membership of national fostering organisations, personalised in-person training, events and awards, a say in plans for the children in their care, and staff support through the week. Each carer also has their own social worker.

If you are thinking about fostering in Stoke-on-Trent, contact Fostering Stoke and two or three of the agencies below, and compare what each offers in training, support and pay.

Sources: Why foster with Stoke (Fostering Stoke, Stoke-on-Trent City Council); Fostering recruitment drive launched to support Stoke-on-Trent teenagers (29 July 2025); Council unveils new promise of support for foster carers (10 June 2025); Higher fostering skills payments mean more children can be cared for locally in Stoke-on-Trent (29 December 2023).
