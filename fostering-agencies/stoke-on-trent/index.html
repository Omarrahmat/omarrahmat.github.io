Fostering in Stoke-on-Trent

Children in care in Stoke-on-Trent are looked after by carers approved by the city council's own service, Fostering Stoke, and by independent agencies such as the services shown above. The council's placement sufficiency strategy, which runs from September 2023 to March 2028, counted 1,109 children in its care in March 2023 and said 69% of them lived in foster care, close to the national average of 70%.

The same strategy explains where the need lies. About a third of the city's foster placements were with independent agencies, and while most children lived in Stoke-on-Trent or within 20 miles, 246 lived further away. In March 2022 the council had 190 approved fostering households. It aims to grow the number of children its own carers can look after by 2% a year, says children aged 10 to 15 are the fastest-growing group in care, and wants to recruit more carers from non-white backgrounds. Fostering Stoke's own pages put it simply: some children are far from their families, and it wants to bring them back to the city.

Fostering Stoke recruits for short-term, long-term, emergency and respite care, short breaks for disabled children, parent and child placements and therapeutic fostering, and keeps brothers and sisters together where it can. In July 2025 it launched Family Pathways, a specialist role offering a full-time home to a teenager moving on from a residential children's home, with a weekly professional fee, an age-based allowance, 20 days of paid holiday, specialist training and a dedicated keyworker. Its Foster Carer Promise of June 2025 adds mentoring and buddying, local support groups, free membership of national fostering organisations, a say in planning for the children in your care, and personalised training.

Fostering with the council means caring for the city's own children, close to their schools and families. An independent agency approves, trains and supports you itself and takes children referred by councils, Stoke-on-Trent among them. The council does not publish a full current pay scale, so ask Fostering Stoke and each agency for their fees and allowances in writing and compare them.
