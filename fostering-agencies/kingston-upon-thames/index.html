Fostering in Kingston upon Thames

Fostering for Kingston's own children is run by Achieving for Children, the not-for-profit company that provides children's services for the Royal Borough of Kingston upon Thames. Its fostering service is shown above with the independent agencies that list Kingston upon Thames as an area they cover.

Achieving for Children Fostering works for three councils, Kingston, Richmond and Windsor and Maidenhead, which own it. It describes its carers as in-house carers, so local children are matched with them first. An independent agency assesses, approves and supports its own carers, and councils turn to agencies when they cannot place a child with their own.

In March 2024 Kingston Council said 40 children were in foster care across the borough and that there is always a need for more carers. It said children need different kinds of care, from emergency and short-term fostering to long-term homes and short breaks, and that fostering can be full-time or only at weekends or in the holidays.

Achieving for Children says its carers have regular one-to-one supervision, group and peer supervision and support groups. Ask each service above what it pays and how it supports carers before you decide. If you live near the edge of the borough, the Richmond upon Thames, Merton, Sutton, Wandsworth and Surrey pages, and the page for London as a whole, list more services nearby.

Sources: About fostering (Achieving for Children Fostering); Shining a light on the positive impact fostering can have on children (Kingston Council, 11 March 2024).
