Fostering in Lambeth

Lambeth Council's own fostering service and the independent agencies that list Lambeth as an area they cover are shown above. The council says a new child comes into its care every day, and many of them need a foster home. Fostering for the council means caring for Lambeth's own children, with its social workers supporting you, and the council says its carers are offered its children first, before it turns to an independent agency. An independent agency assesses, trains and supervises its carers itself.

Lambeth has written down what it promises its carers in a Foster Care Charter, an agreement between the council as corporate parent, its foster carers and the fostering service. The charter recognises that fostering can be isolating and challenging, and sets out the council's ambition for the children it looks after and the support carers can expect in return.

That support includes a dedicated supervising social worker, out-of-hours help from a social worker every day of the year, an induction programme and training throughout the year, in person, one to one or online, a local peer mentoring group, regular support groups and membership of The Fostering Network. The council also lists 14 days of paid respite a year, a Council Tax rebate while you are actively fostering for it, an annual retention payment and a bonus for referring a friend who is approved. It describes its fees and allowances as competitive but does not give the rates on the pages we checked, so ask the council and the agencies above what they pay.

The route in is set out step by step. Lambeth aims to answer an online or email enquiry within two days and to visit you at home within a week. The assessment includes health and criminal record checks alongside the Skills to Foster course, and your application then goes to a panel of independent members, social work professionals, current foster carers and young people who have left care, before the council's lead officer for children's social care makes the final decision.

Speak to the council and two or three of the services above before you choose, and see the Southwark, Wandsworth, Croydon, Merton and Westminster pages, or London as a whole, if you live near the borough's edge.

Sources: Foster for Lambeth (Lambeth Council); What makes Lambeth special? Your support and benefits (Lambeth Council); Training and support (Lambeth Council); Foster Care Charter (Lambeth Council); The fostering process (Lambeth Council).
