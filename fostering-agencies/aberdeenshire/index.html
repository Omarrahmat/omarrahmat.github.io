Fostering in Aberdeenshire

Children in care across Aberdeenshire are looked after by Aberdeenshire Council's fostering service and by the independent agencies listed on this page. In May 2024 the council said more than 350 children were being cared for by foster carers, kinship carers or in residential care, and described an urgent need for more foster carers, particularly for older children and teenagers, unaccompanied asylum-seeking young people, children with additional needs and brothers and sisters who need to stay together.

The council offers interim fostering, which can last up to 24 months while plans are made for a child, long-term and permanent fostering, short breaks, emergency care at short notice, and kinship care. Carers can be approved for children aged 0 to 6, 5 to 12 or 11 to 18. You need to be at least 18 and have a spare room; you do not need to own your home, and you can work outside the home.

Aberdeenshire pays a weekly allowance for each child from April 2026, from £191.13 for a child under five to £307.79 for a young person aged 16 to 18. Each fostering household also receives a yearly skills payment, paid every four weeks, which starts at £10,431.21 for newly approved carers. Our Aberdeenshire allowance page has the full breakdown.

To apply to the council, you register your interest, a social worker visits, and you attend the Skills to Foster course before a home study that can take up to six months. You then meet the fostering panel and hear the decision within 21 days. Comparing the council with two or three of the agencies below is a good way to find the right support for you.

Sources: Aberdeenshire Council highlights need during Foster Care Fortnight; Fostering in Aberdeenshire (Aberdeenshire Council); Becoming a foster carer (Aberdeenshire Council); Aberdeenshire foster carer allowance 2026-27 (Fostering Today).
