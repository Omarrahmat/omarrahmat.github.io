Fostering in Colchester

Children in care in Colchester are looked after by Essex County Council, which runs the fostering service for the whole county, alongside independent agencies such as the services shown above. Essex says its fostering team is rated Outstanding by Ofsted, and Colchester City Council's own announcement notes that the county has hundreds of children who need a safe, stable home.

Essex publishes an example of what its carers receive: for one child aged 11 to 15, an average of £537.12 a week, which it puts at £27,930.24 a year, most of it tax-free. There are extra payments for birthdays, Christmas and summer holiday days out, and expenses for travel to training, family visits and specialist equipment. What you are paid depends on the child and on the service, so ask every service the same question.

Support from the county includes a supervising social worker with a low caseload, a 24-hour line for emergencies, an experienced carer as a buddy from the start of your application, Oasis networks led by experienced carers for breathing space, and an in-house mental health team for carers, their own children and children in care. Carers also have the Essex Foster Carers Association, an independent charity, and membership of The Fostering Network paid by the council.

If you work for Colchester City Council, its fostering friendly policy gives staff who foster, or are being assessed, flexible working and up to nine extra days of paid leave a year for training, meetings and settling a child in. The Essex fostering team also brings an information stand to local events such as Colchester Pride at Firstsite, where you can ask questions with no obligation to apply.

Talk to Essex and to two or three of the services listed here before you choose.

Sources: Why foster with Essex? (Essex County Council); Support for foster carers and your family (Essex County Council); Colchester City Council commits to supporting foster carers with new fostering friendly policy; Find us at Colchester Pride (Essex County Council).
