Fostering in Sefton

Sefton runs along the coast north of Liverpool, from Bootle and Litherland through Crosby and Maghull to Southport. Children in care here are looked after by Sefton Council's own fostering service and by the independent agencies listed on this page. In January 2025 the council said more children were coming into care, that its foster homes were almost full, and that it needed to approve 30 new fostering families during the year to keep up with demand.

The council's fostering pages say applicants need to be over 21 and in good health, that there is no upper age limit, and that being single, your sexuality or your faith are not barriers. It recruits for full-time fostering and for respite care, where a carer looks after a child for short periods, and it has said a child could be living with a new carer in as little as six months from enquiry. Its carers are part of a network of social workers, other carers and trainers, and in January 2024 they launched the Sefton Foster Care Association, a carer-led group that feeds into the council's policies and represents carers at regional meetings.

Sefton takes recruitment out into the borough. During Foster Care Fortnight in May 2025 its team held stalls in supermarkets in Maghull, Southport and Litherland and at The Strand in Bootle, an information evening in Crosby and a coastal walk from Hightown to Crosby.

If you live in Sefton, talk to the council and to two or three of the agencies on this page, and ask each about the children they most need homes for, the training and support you would get, and what they pay.

Sources: Fostering (Sefton Council); Is 2025 the year you become a foster carer? (Sefton Council, January 2025); Sefton Council's foster carers launch Sefton Foster Care Association (Sefton Council, January 2024); Sefton celebrates Foster Care Fortnight 2025 (Sefton Council, 13 May 2025).
