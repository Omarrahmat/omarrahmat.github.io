Fostering in North Tyneside

In North Tyneside, children in care are looked after by North Tyneside Council's own fostering service, working alongside the services shown on this page. Ofsted judged the council's children's services outstanding in all five areas of its inspection in November 2024. The council describes a range of fostering roles, from full-time and long-term care to short breaks for disabled children, emergency care, and assessment and remand placements.

The council's fostering strategy for 2022 to 2025 says it has a particular need for carers skilled at looking after teenagers, and for carers who can keep brothers and sisters together. It also notes that the number of local foster carers has fallen in recent years, and that where possible it wants children to live with local carers so they stay connected to their communities.

On support, the council describes weekly fees paid 52 weeks a year under a skills-based structure, an age-related allowance for each child, extra fees for caring for two or more children, and two weeks of paid holiday a year, or four for specialist carers. Carers can join a buddy scheme, support groups and the Mockingbird programme, and an out-of-hours service is available.

To start, the council points enquirers to the Foster with North East website and says its team supports applicants throughout the application. Before deciding, it is worth comparing what the council offers with two or three of the agencies on this page, asking each about training, support and pay for the kind of fostering you have in mind.
