Fostering in Bolton

Bolton Council runs its own fostering service alongside the independent agencies listed on this page. In March 2026 it launched its Bolton Legends campaign to encourage more local people to open their homes to children who need care, and in February 2025 it introduced the Mockingbird model with The Fostering Network, in which an experienced hub home carer supports a small group of fostering households with sleepovers, advice and social activities. The first group linked seven households.

The council pays an allowance for each child, based on age, to cover day-to-day costs, together with a professional fee that is also linked to the child's age. Extra allowances are paid for clothing, holidays, birthdays and festivals, and carers receive a retainer for up to eight weeks a year when they have no child living with them.

Before approval, applicants complete Skills to Foster training; after approval there is a two-year programme of seven core skills courses. Each carer has a supervising social worker who visits at least every six weeks, an experienced buddy during the first year, support groups including groups for male carers and Asian carers, and a 24-hour helpline.

To apply you need to be over 21 with a spare room, and couples should have been together for at least two years. Renting is fine if your tenancy is secure, and driving helps but is not essential. Compare the council with two or three of the agencies below before you decide.

Sources: Bolton Legends foster campaign aims to inspire more foster carers (Bolton Council); Mockingbird lands in Bolton (Bolton Council); Fostering training and support (Bolton Council); Can you foster? (Bolton Council).
