Fostering in Blackburn with Darwen

Children in care in Blackburn with Darwen are looked after by Blackburn with Darwen Council's fostering service and by the independent agencies shown above. The council recruits through Foster with Us, a hub it shares with other North West councils; in May 2025, at the start of Foster Care Fortnight, the hub set out to find 16 more foster carers that year, and the council's lead member for children said it is always looking for more.

When it marked Foster Care Fortnight in 2023, the council said it urgently needed more carers and listed what it offers: an allowance that depends on the carer's skill level and the child's age, a training programme that includes its Journey to Foster course, a dedicated social worker with regular meetings, and support groups for carers and their families. It does not give the amounts in these announcements, so ask when you enquire.

The council has also run a specialist scheme, Time 4 Change, based on Multi-dimensional Treatment Foster Care, in which carers looked after school-age children with complex needs for a short time with intensive support from a dedicated team and met each week to share advice. That description dates from 2015, so ask whether the scheme still runs if specialist fostering interests you.

Our Blackburn, Lancashire and North West pages cover the services working nearby. Speak to the council through Foster with Us and to two or three of the services above about the children they most need homes for, their training and the support they offer.
