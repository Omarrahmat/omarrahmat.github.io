Fostering in Bradford

Fostering for children in care in Bradford is run by Bradford Children and Families Trust under the name Foster for Bradford. The Trust registered with Ofsted as a fostering agency in April 2023, under a trust arrangement with the council. The independent agencies shown above also recruit and support carers across the district.

Ofsted inspected Foster for Bradford from 8 to 11 September 2025 and rated it good overall and in each of the three areas it judges, up from requires improvement to be good in March 2024. At the time, 443 approved foster carers were looking after 566 children and young people. Inspectors found that more carers were being recruited, which they said should let more children stay local, and that carer assessments were thorough and had improved. They asked the service to make sure new carers show they have met the national training standards within 12 months of approval, or 18 months for family and friends carers.

The council has said it gives priority to assessing people who will care for older children and teenagers, and part-time carers who can help look after disabled children, and that you need at least one spare room. Bradford also uses the Mockingbird model, in which an experienced hub carer supports a small group of fostering households; in 2023 it was opening two new groups and asked experienced carers with two spare beds to run them. Ofsted said the model continues to give carers good support.

Fostering with the Trust means caring for Bradford's own children, usually close to home, with a supervising social worker from the Trust; the council has said that more carers working directly with it helps it keep children in the district. An independent agency approves, trains and supports you itself and takes children referred by councils, Bradford among them. You can reach Foster for Bradford at fostering@bradford.gov.uk or on 01274 435100; talk to it and to two or three of the services above before you choose.
