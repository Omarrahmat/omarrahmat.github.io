Fostering in Camden

Camden Council runs its own fostering service, and it is shown above alongside the independent agencies that list Camden as an area they cover. The council says it is actively recruiting more foster carers, and that its own carers are the first to be contacted when a Camden child needs a foster home.

To foster for Camden you need to be over 25. Single people, couples, same-sex couples, people with or without children of their own, and people who live outside the borough can all apply. A spare bedroom is not essential: without one you can be approved for children aged up to two, and with one for children of any age up to 18.

The council puts the whole process at around six months. It starts with a phone call and an application form, followed by a home visit and background checks (medical, police and employment), a three-day Skills to Foster course, and then at least eight visits from an assessing social worker before your report goes to an independent fostering panel.

Once approved, Camden carers have an allocated social worker, a 24-hour duty line, therapeutic help from the local child and adolescent mental health service, support groups, and training shared with other north London boroughs in the North London Fostering Consortium. The council also lists extras such as no council tax bill for carers living in Camden, retention payments at set milestones, free parking in residents' bays and a laptop for fostering paperwork.

If you live in or near Camden, speak to the council and to two or three of the services above, and ask each about fees, training and day-to-day support before you decide.

Sources: Become a foster carer (Camden Council); Who can be a foster carer? (Camden Council); Benefits for foster carers (Camden Council); Why foster with us? (Camden Council).
