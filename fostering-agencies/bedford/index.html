Fostering in Bedford

Children in care from Bedford are looked after by Bedford Borough Council's own fostering service and by the independent agencies shown above. The council is one of 11 East of England councils that recruit together through Foster East, a Department for Education funded partnership. When Foster East launched, Bedford Borough said more than 250 children and young people in the borough, from babies to 18-year-olds, needed homes.

The council approves its mainstream carers for children up to 18 (from age five where someone in the home smokes or vapes) and most carers are approved for more than one kind of care: long-term homes, short-term care while plans are made, planned respite such as a weekend, emergency care for a night or a few days, specialist care for children whose behaviour is more challenging, and parent and child placements. Young people in long-term care can stay on to 21 under Staying Put if everyone agrees.

Bedford started the Mockingbird programme in March 2025. Groups of six to ten fostering households form a constellation around an experienced hub carer, who offers sleepovers, advice and a monthly get-together, and the council says three constellations with 24 families are now running. Its carers also get a supervising social worker, a buddy carer, Skills to Foster training, support groups, free membership of The Fostering Network and the Bedfordshire Foster Care Association, and free gym and swim access at the local GLL leisure centres.

For the wider county, including Central Bedfordshire's published allowances, see our Bedfordshire page. Before you apply, it is worth talking to the council and two or three of the agencies above, and comparing the training, local support and fees each offers.

Sources: Types of fostering (Bedford Borough Council); Mockingbird (Bedford Borough Council); East of England local authorities collaborate to appeal for more foster families (Bedford Borough Council); Foster carer support (Bedford Borough Council); Foster families being celebrated as part of Foster Care Fortnight (Bedford Borough Council, 2025).
