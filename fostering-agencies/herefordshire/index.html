Fostering in Herefordshire

Herefordshire Council said in May 2025 that it urgently needs more foster carers. It wants children to live with local families who reflect their background and culture and, wherever possible, stay near their own families, schools and communities. The council's own service sits alongside the independent agencies listed on this page.

The council offers short-term, long-term, emergency and respite fostering, parent and child placements, care for unaccompanied asylum-seeking children, supported lodgings, specialist fostering and overnight short breaks for disabled children. Short-break carers can be paid £119 a night.

For mainstream fostering, the council says carers receive a fee based on their skill level and an allowance for each child, together worth up to £790 a week, plus a full Council Tax exemption. It describes itself as a not-for-profit service and offers 24-hour support, a dedicated fostering social worker, local induction and training, and social events and peer support groups. Its pages do not say which year the rates apply to, so check current figures when you enquire.

You need to be over 21, with no upper age limit, and have a spare bedroom, but you do not need to own your home or hold any qualifications. Many carers keep working, and single people and couples of any gender can apply. Talk to the council and to two or three of the agencies below to compare what each offers in your part of the county.

Sources: Urgent need for foster carers in Herefordshire (Herefordshire Council, May 2025); Could you foster? (Herefordshire Council); Why foster with Herefordshire (Herefordshire Council); Overnight short breaks for children with disabilities (Herefordshire Council).
