Fostering in Wokingham

Children in care from Wokingham borough are looked after by Wokingham Borough Council's own fostering team and by independent agencies, including those shown above. The council says it urgently needs more local carers so that children can stay close to their families, friends and schools, and that it always looks first for homes with its own carers in places such as Wokingham town, Woodley, Twyford, Shinfield, Arborfield and Crowthorne. It has asked in particular for empty nesters with a spare room, and for experienced carers who would like to transfer from another service.

Its guidance says you need to be at least 21, have some life experience, have a spare room and live within twenty miles of the borough. Age, culture, sexual orientation and whether you own or rent do not matter, and single people and couples can both apply. You can work as long as your hours are flexible or you have help at home, because carers need to be around for some school hours, holidays, training and meetings. The council offers emergency and weekend care as well as longer placements that support a young person until they are ready to live independently.

Council carers receive a fee that rises as they complete more training, an allowance for each child and help with council tax. In May 2025 the council said fees started from £140 a week for each child, with extra allowances for summer activities, birthdays and Christmas, and that an experienced level two carer looking after two children all year could earn more than £25,000. Carers also get local training, 24-hour support and social events.

Since July 2024 Wokingham has been one of 20 councils in Local Authority Fostering South East, a hub funded by the Department for Education. When the hub opened, the councils said the region had more than 11,000 children in care and fewer than 3,000 council foster carers. If you live in or near the borough, talk to the council and to two or three of the agencies above, and compare their training, support and pay.

Sources: Fostering in Wokingham (Wokingham Borough Council); Fostering FAQs (Wokingham Borough Council, updated 30 September 2024); Foster Care Fortnight: how relationships make a difference to young people (Wokingham Borough Council, May 2025); New fostering hub launched (Wokingham Borough Council, 18 July 2024).
