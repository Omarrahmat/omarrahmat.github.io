Fostering in Enfield

Children in Enfield's care live with carers approved by the council's own fostering service or by independent agencies such as those on this page. The council's fostering statement of purpose for 2024 to 2027 says that, where possible, it tries to keep children close to their families, friends, schools and GP, and that it gives priority to recruiting carers who live in or near the borough. Since 1 October 2026, Enfield's fostering recruitment has been handled by the North London Fostering Hub, led by Camden for six boroughs including Enfield.

Enfield approves carers for short-term and long-term fostering (with Staying Put possible up to 21), respite, parent and child placements, and family and friends care. Applicants must be over 21 and living in the UK; single people and unmarried couples in long-term relationships are welcome. Preparation is a four-day Skills to Foster programme, and approved carers complete four training events a year plus development days twice a year.

Each carer has a named supervising social worker, there are support groups every two months, and the council pays for membership of the Fostering Network, which gives access to independent advice. Before applying, you can ask for a one-to-one conversation with an existing Enfield foster carer.

Enfield pays a weekly maintenance allowance for the child and a skills fee for the carer, in five age bands, with extra payments for birthdays, festivals and holidays and higher rates where a child's needs call for them. Its fostering site says allowances can reach over £450 a week but does not publish the full rates, so ask the council and two or three of the agencies below for their figures and compare the support each offers.

Sources: Fostering Statement of Purpose 2024-27 (Enfield Council); Why foster with Enfield Council; How we support you to foster (Enfield Council); Camden chosen to lead new North London Fostering Hub (Camden Council); Become a foster carer (Haringey Council); Fostering Today: six north London councils launch a shared fostering hub.
