Fostering in Watford

The independent fostering agencies shown above list Watford as an area they cover. Children in care in Watford are the responsibility of Hertfordshire County Council, which also runs its own fostering service across the county, with its south and west teams based at Hemel Hempstead.

Hertfordshire's fostering statement of purpose for 2024-25 says that on 31 March 2024 the council had 322 mainstream and 81 family and friends fostering households, caring for 453 children between them. It looks for carers for babies through to teenagers, including children with additional needs and large groups of brothers and sisters who need to stay together. Its fostering strategy for 2023 to 2025 aimed to approve 60 new fostering households a year, and the carers it consulted asked for more short-break carers, particularly for teenagers. In May 2025 the council launched a campaign asking schools, businesses and community groups to help, saying it has around 1,000 children in its care and not enough foster carers.

The council offers emergency, short and long-term, sleepover and parent and child fostering, as well as short breaks for disabled children. Each carer has a supervising social worker and is given an experienced carer as a buddy on approval, and the council runs Mockingbird hubs, a trauma-informed support team and an evening out-of-hours line. Carers approved with an agency can move to the council through a fast-track transfer.

Compare the agencies above with the council before you decide. The Hertfordshire page lists more services across the county.

Sources: Statement of Purpose of the Fostering Service 2024-25 (Hertfordshire County Council, April 2024); Hertfordshire's In-house Fostering Strategy 2023-2025 (Hertfordshire County Council, July 2023); Fostering: transfer to us (Hertfordshire County Council); New campaign calls for help in recruiting more foster carers (Hertfordshire Grid for Learning, 21 May 2025).
