Fostering in Doncaster

Children in care in Doncaster live with carers approved by the local authority service, Foster for Doncaster, or by independent fostering agencies such as those listed on this page. Foster for Doncaster recruits for emergency care at short notice, short-term care lasting weeks or months, respite breaks from a single night to a few weeks, and long-term care for children who cannot go back to their birth families. It also offers targeted placements, including parent and child assessments and same-day emergency care, and supports family and friends (kinship) carers.

Each Doncaster carer has a supervising social worker who visits at least every four weeks, with social work support available around the clock. New carers are paired with an experienced buddy, and the service runs Mockingbird hubs that link up to ten fostering households. Carers can join the Doncaster Foster Carers Association, a carer-led charity, free of charge. Training starts with the three-day Skills to Foster course before approval and continues through three skill levels.

The council's fostering pages list a weekly allowance for each child, from £159 for children aged up to 4 to £233 for those over 16, plus a weekly skills fee of £100, £150 or £267 depending on the carer's level, and £300 a week for targeted placements. Holiday, festival and activity grants are paid on top.

The council says the journey from first enquiry to approval usually takes around six months. Before you decide, speak to Foster for Doncaster and to two or three of the agencies below, and compare the training, support and pay each one offers.

Sources: All about fostering (City of Doncaster Council); How we will look after you (City of Doncaster Council); Foster carer training and development (City of Doncaster Council); Steps to becoming a foster carer (City of Doncaster Council); Why foster for Doncaster (City of Doncaster Council).
