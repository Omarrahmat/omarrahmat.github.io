Fostering in Suffolk

Children in care in Suffolk are looked after by Suffolk Fostering Service, run by Suffolk County Council, and by the independent agencies listed on this page. Suffolk is one of 11 councils in Foster East, a regional fostering hub for the East of England.

The council says it is not looking for perfect families but for real people with a sense of fun, patience and commitment, who believe children can reach their potential. You do not need to own your home, be in a relationship, work full time, have formal qualifications or have children of your own, but you do need a spare bedroom.

Suffolk sets out ten steps from first enquiry to approval. After an information event and a home visit, the first stage of checks and references takes about 30 to 60 days. You then attend Skills to Foster training, and the second stage, in which a social worker visits regularly and writes your assessment report, takes about two to four months before an independent panel considers your approval.

Council carers receive payments set by tier, up to 21 nights of paid respite, a dedicated social worker, an out-of-hours helpline, local support groups and therapeutic support, and Mockingbird groups are being set up around the county. Agencies set their own fees and support, so compare the council with two or three of the agencies below before you decide.

Sources: Why foster with Suffolk? (Suffolk County Council); How to become a foster carer (Suffolk County Council).
