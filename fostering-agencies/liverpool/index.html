Fostering in Liverpool

Liverpool City Council runs its own not-for-profit fostering service, and the independent agencies shown above also approve carers in the city. The council says its children's services look after more than 1,500 children. Fostering for the council means caring for Liverpool children, helping them stay close to their friends, schools and communities, with the council's social workers behind you; an independent agency assesses, approves and supervises you itself and offers homes to children placed by councils.

Liverpool recruits through Foster4, launched in May 2024 with Cheshire East, Cheshire West and Chester, Halton, Knowsley, St Helens, Warrington and Wirral and backed by the Department for Education. At launch the eight councils had more than 4,600 children in care between them and just over 1,150 approved council foster carers, and said more were urgently needed. Government funding announced in December 2023 put over £1 million into the Foster4 recruitment hub and a further £1 million into extending the Mockingbird programme, in which an experienced hub carer supports six to ten nearby fostering families. Liverpool's first Mockingbird group, launched in December 2024, brought together ten foster families.

The council's latest commissioning and sufficiency strategy, for 2025 to 2030, centres on stable homes for children in or near the city, and it quotes Ofsted's 2023 finding that some children wait too long for a home that meets their needs.

After an enquiry, an application and a home visit, applicants take the Skills to Foster course and a two-stage assessment, with the assessing social worker visiting about seven times, before a fostering panel, which meets at least monthly. Foster4 aims to approve carers within four to six months of Skills to Foster. Carers already with an agency or another council can transfer in about eight to twelve weeks, and the council says it can often match current fees.

Council carers get Council Tax exemption, training before and after approval, free Lifestyles gym membership and use of a holiday home at Presthaven Beach Resort, alongside skills and children's allowance payments. We do not yet have a Liverpool allowance page, so ask the council and two or three of the agencies above for their rates. If you live near the city's edge, the Knowsley, Sefton and Wirral pages list more services.
