Fostering in Milton Keynes

Children in care in Milton Keynes are looked after by Milton Keynes City Council's fostering service and by the independent agencies listed on this page. In May 2026 the council said it had 120 foster carers looking after 185 children, from babies and sibling groups to teenagers and children with complex needs, and that it needs more carers for both short and long-term placements so that children can stay close to home.

The council offers short-term, long-term, emergency, parent and child and respite fostering, and supported lodgings for young people aged 16 to 21 who are getting ready to live independently. It says it needs more carers for unaccompanied asylum-seeking children, and has a constant need for supported lodgings hosts. You can be single, married or in a couple, renting or owning your home, as long as you are at least 21 and have a spare bedroom. Parenting experience helps but is not essential, and many carers keep working.

Under its 2026/27 payment scheme, Milton Keynes pays a weekly allowance for each child from £203 for a child under five to £298 for a 16 or 17 year old, plus a weekly professional fee per child that starts at £169.50 for newly approved carers and rises through the tiers. The council also mentions specialist training, a mentoring scheme and support around the clock. Our Milton Keynes allowance page has the full figures, including birthday, festival and holiday payments.

Agencies set their own fees and support, so speak to the council and two or three of the agencies below, and ask each about training and out-of-hours help, before you choose.

Sources: Foster Care Fortnight: answering your questions about fostering (Milton Keynes City Council); Types of fostering (Milton Keynes City Council); Milton Keynes foster carer allowance 2026-27 (Fostering Today).
