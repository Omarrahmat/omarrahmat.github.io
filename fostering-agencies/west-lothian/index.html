Fostering in West Lothian

Children in care in West Lothian are looked after by West Lothian Council's Family Based Care Service and by the independent agencies listed on this page. The council offers short-break fostering as well as longer placements, and an intensive fostering scheme for children, usually aged 10 and over, whose needs are significant and complex.

The council welcomes applicants of any sexual orientation and any ethnic or cultural background, whether single, married or living together, with or without children of their own, renting or owning, working or not, and people who grew up in care themselves. The firm requirements are that you are over 21, have a spare bedroom and are willing to go through checks and a full assessment. Carers already approved elsewhere can also apply.

West Lothian publishes its rates. From 1 April 2025 its fee was £238.82 per child per week, paid every four weeks, on top of a weekly allowance for the child's costs that rises with age, from £168.31 for a child up to four to £268.41 for a young person aged 16 to 18. Carers also receive an extra week's allowance at Christmas and for the child's birthday, and two weeks for holidays, and short-break carers get £300 for the summer school holiday. Check the council's page for the current year's figures.

Local carers have told the council's news team that you just need room in your heart; one Fauldhouse couple have looked after close to 50 children over nearly two decades. Speak to the council and two or three of the agencies below, and compare training and support, before choosing.

Sources: Foster with us (West Lothian Council); Fostering criteria (West Lothian Council); Fees and allowances (West Lothian Council); Change lives through fostering with West Lothian Council (West Lothian Council news).
