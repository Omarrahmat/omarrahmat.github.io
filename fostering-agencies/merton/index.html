Fostering in Merton

Two fostering services list Merton as an area they cover, and both are shown above: Merton Council's own fostering service and an independent agency. Fostering with the council means looking after children from the borough, supported by the council's social workers; an independent agency assesses, approves and supports its own carers, and councils ask agencies to find homes when they cannot place a child with their own carers.

Merton says Ofsted has rated its services Outstanding, and it sets out what its carers receive: fees and allowances through the year, with extra payments for birthdays, religious festivals and holidays, a £500 start-up grant, a supervising social worker of your own, out-of-hours support every day of the year, an ongoing training programme, a buddy scheme, a local foster carers' support group and a paid Fostering Network membership. Its carers can also be part of the Mockingbird Family Model, a support network for fostering households, and the council says its carers get first call on its placements ahead of any other agency or council.

The council puts the journey at six to nine months. After an enquiry and a home visit comes a three-and-a-half-day preparation course, then an application with references, a medical and background checks, a two-stage assessment that looks closely at your history, relationships and lifestyle, a fostering panel, and a final decision. When Merton last ran fostering roadshows, it said it most wanted carers for brothers and sisters, teenagers and unaccompanied asylum-seeking children, and that renting, working part-time or claiming benefits need not rule anyone out.

Talk to both services above before you decide, and ask each what it pays and how it supports carers. If you live near the borough's edges, the Croydon, Sutton, South London and London pages list more services nearby.

Sources: Why choose us? Fostering with Merton (Merton Council); The fostering process (Merton Council); Could you be a foster carer? (Merton Council newsroom, 8 May 2019).
