Fostering in Sunderland

In Sunderland, the council's fostering service is provided by Together for Children, Sunderland City Council's children's services partner. Children in care live with its carers or with independent agencies such as those listed below.

Sunderland is also home to Foster with North East, the regional recruitment hub that Together for Children leads for twelve councils from Darlington and Durham to Northumberland and Stockton-on-Tees. The hub sits at the Sandhill Centre in the city centre and is staffed by a small team of fostering social workers. Ring its free number or fill in its online form and, during opening hours, you should hear back the same day; the hub keeps in touch while your local council assesses you and can pair you with an experienced buddy carer.

The hub was set up because carers are scarce. Its own figures put the number of children in care across the region at about 6,000, against roughly 1,500 foster carers. In its first year more than 850 people enquired, and the council reports that the time from application to approval fell to 4.8 months, against a national average of six months.

Research the hub publicised found many people wrongly believe you must be married, have children, work full time or earn at least £25,000 to foster; none of these is a requirement. Each council sets its own allowances, so ask Together for Children and two or three of the agencies below what they pay and how they support carers.

Sources: Trailblazing fostering hub celebrates its first birthday (Sunderland City Council, October 2024); What will happen when you make an enquiry with us (Foster with North East); Sunderland leads on huge drive to recruit foster carers as demand rises (Sunderland Echo).
