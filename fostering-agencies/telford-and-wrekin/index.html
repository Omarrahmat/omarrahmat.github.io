Fostering in Telford and Wrekin

Children in care in Telford, Wellington, Newport and the surrounding villages are looked after by Telford & Wrekin Council's own fostering service and by the agencies listed on this page. The council says it needs many more fostering households so that local children can stay close to home, and it offers part-time fostering, such as a few days a month or respite care, as well as full-time placements that can last from a week to adulthood.

You need to be at least 21, with no upper age limit, and have a spare room for a child. You can be single, in a relationship, married or in a civil partnership, with or without children of your own, renting or owning, working or retired; no professional qualifications are needed, and you can keep a job that is flexible enough for a child's needs. Assessment includes a DBS check, a medical and personal references.

The council says it pays competitive fees and allowances, and in 2023 it raised its fees and allowances, its birthday, festival and holiday payments, and let carers claim back extra travel costs; it does not publish the weekly figures online, so ask for them. Support includes a supervising social worker, preparation courses and ongoing training, mentoring and peer support, an annual conference and in-house therapeutic help, including a clinician carers can call day or night.

The council holds regular drop-in information events, often at Ketley Community Centre, along with online sessions and walk-and-talks at The Wrekin and Telford Town Park. As agencies set their own fees and support, it is worth comparing the council with two or three of the services below.

Sources: Fostering frequently asked questions (Telford & Wrekin Council); Find out more about fostering with Telford & Wrekin Council (8 May 2026); Fostering with Telford & Wrekin Council keeps children local (14 May 2024); Foster with Telford and Wrekin Council (14 February 2025); Increased financial support for council's foster carers (Telford & Wrekin Council, March 2023).
