Fostering in Great Yarmouth

In Great Yarmouth, children in care are the responsibility of Norfolk County Council. They live with carers approved by the council's Norfolk Fostering Service or by independent agencies such as the services shown above. The council has a local fostering office in Great Yarmouth, as well as in King's Lynn, Thetford and Norwich.

Norfolk says five children come into its care every week and that it urgently needs more foster carers, for children of every age, so that children in care can stay close to home. Its cabinet member for children's services has said the county looks after about 1,200 children. Some need a family for a few weeks; others for much longer.

The Norfolk Fostering Service describes itself as a not-for-profit service within Children's Services, rated outstanding by Ofsted, with more than 300 foster families. New carers are given a therapist and a supervising social worker for their first two years, an experienced carer as a mentor, support around the clock, agreed sleepover respite, a daily duty worker, thank-you payments for long service, and help if an allegation is made. Training starts before approval on the Skills to Foster course, which uses virtual reality headsets, and continues with free local courses in person and online. Every carer gets family membership of The Fostering Network.

You do not need qualifications. The council says that if you are over 21 there is no upper age limit, and that you can be single or in a couple, rent or own your home, work, have pets and have children of your own. Norfolk is part of Foster East, a network of eleven councils in the East of England.

Ask the council and two or three of the services listed here what they pay, how they train you, and how they support carers living on the coast.

Sources: Why foster for the council (Norfolk County Council); Training and support (Norfolk County Council); Make next Christmas a family Christmas for a child in care (Norfolk County Council); Norfolk County Council foster carers plea (Great Yarmouth Mercury).
