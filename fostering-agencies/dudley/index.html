Fostering in Dudley

Dudley Council runs its own fostering service, Fostering in Dudley. In May 2026 the council said more than 500 children were in care across the borough and that it had nearly 80 foster carers, and it asked for at least 60 more people to come forward so that more children can stay close to their schools, friends and support networks. Children from Dudley may also live with independent agencies such as those listed on this page.

The council says you do not need to own your home or have a traditional family set-up to foster. Its carers look after children of all ages, from young children to teenagers, on short and long-term placements, and some care for brothers and sisters together.

New carers get full training and financial support, with ongoing guidance and support available 24 hours a day, every day of the year. The council holds drop-in and information sessions where you can speak to the team and to carers who already foster for Dudley.

Before deciding, compare what the council offers with two or three of the agencies below, and ask each about pay, training and support in the Dudley borough.

Sources: Dudley calls for 60 more foster carers as local heroes celebrated during Foster Care Fortnight (Dudley Council); Dudley's Mayor and Mayoress encourage people to consider fostering (Dudley Council).
