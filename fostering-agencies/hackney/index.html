Fostering in Hackney

Hackney Council looks after its own children in care, and the services listed above also take enquiries from people in the borough. Since October 2026 anyone who wants to foster for Hackney starts with the North London Fostering Hub, which the council joined that month. The hub is a partnership of Barnet, Camden, Enfield, Hackney, Haringey and Islington, hosted by Camden, and it aims to make registering to foster simpler and more consistent across the six boroughs. Through the hub you can check whether you can foster, speak to someone about it and register your interest.

The hub grows out of the North London Fostering and Permanency Consortium, which has linked the same six councils since 2012. When the hub was announced, Camden said the Room Makers programme, which funds changes to carers' homes to create extra space, would be extended to every fostering hub, which may help if a lack of room is what holds you back.

Camden's announcement makes the point that you may be able to foster whether you are single, married or in a relationship, working or retired. Hackney has a long record of trying new approaches: in 2013 it was one of six councils in The Fostering Network's Head, Heart, Hands pilot, which brought social pedagogy into foster care.

Speak to the hub and to two or three of the services above before you decide, and see the pages for Islington, Haringey and Enfield, or London as a whole, if you live near the borough's edge.

Sources: Fostering with Hackney (Hackney Council); Camden chosen to lead new North London Fostering Hub (Camden Council); Hackney's approach to fostering shapes national pilot project (Hackney Council, May 2013).
