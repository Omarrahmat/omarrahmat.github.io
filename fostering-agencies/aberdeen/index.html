Fostering in Aberdeen

Children in care in Aberdeen are looked after by Aberdeen City Council's foster care team and by independent fostering agencies, including those shown above. The city council is separate from Aberdeenshire Council, which runs its own fostering service for the surrounding towns and countryside.

During Foster Care Fortnight in May 2024 the city council published figures from April that year: 73 fostering households, made up of 128 foster carers, were looking after 83 children and young people, while 139 Aberdeen children were living with carers from external fostering agencies. The council said it wanted more carers in the city so that children can stay in their own communities, because Aberdeen children are often placed in other parts of Scotland.

The council says you need to be over 21 and have the right to work in the UK to apply. Single people, married couples and partners can all foster, whatever their sexual orientation, and renters can foster as well as homeowners. It looks for carers who are nurturing and who understand how trauma and difficult early experiences affect children, and its team supports carers once they are approved.

If you live in Aberdeen, it is worth talking to the council and to two or three of the agencies above, and asking each about the training, round-the-clock support and payments they offer for the children you could care for.

Sources: Foster Care Fortnight 2024 (Aberdeen City Council).
