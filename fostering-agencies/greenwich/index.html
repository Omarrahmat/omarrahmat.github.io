Fostering in Greenwich

If you live in or near Greenwich, you can foster for the Royal Borough of Greenwich or for one of the independent agencies shown above. The council's own carers look after the borough's children; an agency approves and supports its own carers and takes children that councils across London and beyond ask it to place.

The council's strategy for children in care for 2023 to 2026 gives the picture at 31 March 2023: 414 children in care, 174 of them with the council's own foster carers and 134 with agency carers. The share living with the council's own carers had been rising, and the strategy aims to increase it further, to recruit more of its own carers, and to find more foster homes for children with special educational needs and for those needing more intensive care. It also notes that more children from other councils live in Greenwich than Greenwich children do. In January 2024 the council said it hoped to find around 200 new fostering homes that year, and in May 2026, with over 400 children in its care, it said it urgently needed more local carers.

Short-term fostering can last a few weeks and long-term fostering months or years. The council has also tried new routes: in March 2025 it began a one-year pilot with Now Foster to find Weekenders, people who give a child in care a settled home to visit at weekends. In September 2025 it became a Fostering Friendly Employer, giving its own staff who foster up to five extra days of paid leave a year for training, panel and review meetings.

Its carers get support around the clock, a supervising social worker, training and mentoring, support groups and counselling, membership of The Fostering Network and the local foster carers' association, and the chance to join a Mockingbird group. To foster for the council you need to be over 18, live within 20 miles of the borough, have a spare room and be in good health; you can rent or own, be single or in a couple, and LGBTQIA+ applicants are welcome. Smokers may foster only if they smoke outside, and not children under five. The council checks your health, references, employment and an enhanced DBS before approval.

Ask the council and two or three of the services above what they pay and how they would support you. Our Lewisham, Bexley, Bromley and South London pages list more services nearby.
