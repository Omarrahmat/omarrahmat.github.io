Fostering in Greenwich

The Royal Borough of Greenwich runs its own fostering service for the borough's children, and it is shown above with the independent agencies that list Greenwich as an area they cover. Councils look first to their own carers and ask agencies to find homes when they cannot place a child locally, so carers with either can end up caring for Greenwich children.

The need is large. In January 2024 the council said it had more than 400 children and young people in its care and was aiming for around 200 new fostering homes that year, so that children could stay near their schools, friends and support networks. By September 2025 it said 417 young people were in its care and that it urgently needed more carers. It offers short-term fostering, which can last a few weeks, and long-term fostering for months or years, and its services for children and young people were rated Outstanding by Ofsted in 2024.

Greenwich has tried new ways to find homes. In March 2025 it began a one-year pilot with Now Foster to recruit Weekenders, people who give a child in care a regular, settled home to visit at weekends; the council expected to recruit four in the pilot. In September 2025 it became a Fostering Friendly Employer, giving its own staff who foster up to five extra days of paid leave a year for training, panel and review meetings.

If you work for a large employer, ask whether it has a similar policy. Speak to the agencies above and to the council before you choose, and ask each about training, support and what it pays. The Lewisham, Bexley, Bromley and London pages list more services nearby.

Sources: Change a child's life in 2024 (Royal Borough of Greenwich, 22 January 2024); New innovative partnership to recruit more foster carers (Royal Borough of Greenwich, 10 April 2025); Increasing support for Royal Borough of Greenwich staff who are foster carers (15 September 2025).
