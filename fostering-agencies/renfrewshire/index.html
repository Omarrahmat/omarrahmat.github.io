Fostering in Renfrewshire

Renfrewshire Council fosters for children in care across the council area, and its own service is shown above with the independent agencies that cover Renfrewshire. The council offers stay-over fostering for a night or a weekend, interim fostering for anything from a few days to several months, and long-term fostering until a young person is 18. Carers may look after babies, teenagers, brothers and sisters, disabled children or unaccompanied asylum-seeking children, and the council says keeping siblings together wherever possible is a priority under the Promise.

The council asks that you are over 21, in good health and have a spare bedroom. You can be working, self-employed or not working, single, married or living together, of any sexual orientation or faith, and you can rent or own your home.

Renfrewshire's allowances page sets out a weekly fostering payment of £330.46 per child, plus an allowance by age: £178.56 for a child aged 0 to 4, £207.73 for 5 to 15 and £284.75 for 16 and 17, with £206 a week for young people in continuing care from 18 to 21. The page does not state the year, so check current rates with the team. Carers who are already with an agency can transfer to the council.

Becoming a carer runs in three stages: information, six compulsory preparation modules with home visits, and a home study assessment that the council says takes 6 to 9 months before panel. Approved carers get a supervising social worker, training, access to an educational psychology team, a local carers' network and free Fostering Network membership. Speak to two or three of the services above before you decide.
