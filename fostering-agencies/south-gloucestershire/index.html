Fostering in South Gloucestershire

South Gloucestershire wraps around the north and east of Bristol, taking in Yate, Chipping Sodbury, Thornbury, Kingswood and Bradley Stoke. Children in care here are looked after by South Gloucestershire Council's fostering service, Foster South Glos, and by the independent agencies listed on this page. During Foster Care Fortnight in May 2024 the council set out to recruit 15 new foster carers across the area, marking it with a group of runners at the Pomphrey Hill parkrun.

The council says it wants to hear from anyone aged 21 or over with a spare bedroom, and that placements can be anything from a single night to several years. Its carers receive a weekly payment to cover the cost of caring, a dedicated fostering team from first enquiry to approval, and ongoing training. Earlier council appeals have said that a long-term tenancy is enough (you do not need to own your home), that single people and same-sex couples can foster, and that applicants are considered whatever their age.

Teenagers have long been a priority. In 2015 the council said 86 teenagers were in its care and asked especially for people who could give stable homes to older children, brothers and sisters, and children with disabilities, while making clear that babies, toddlers and primary-age children need carers too.

If you live in South Gloucestershire, speak to the council and to two or three of the agencies on this page. Ask which children each most needs homes for now, what support is available day and night, and what they pay, before you choose.

Sources: Park runners highlight number of new foster families needed in South Glos (South Gloucestershire Council, 21 May 2024); Appeal for people to consider fostering teenagers (South Gloucestershire Council, 2 February 2015).
