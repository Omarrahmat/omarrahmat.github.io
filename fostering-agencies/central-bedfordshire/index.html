Fostering in Central Bedfordshire

Central Bedfordshire Council is responsible for every child who comes into care in its area, and its own fostering service, rated good by Ofsted, is shown above with the independent agencies that cover the district. The council works with neighbouring councils through the Foster East partnership.

The council fosters in many ways: short-term care, including emergencies and out-of-hours stays of usually up to 72 hours; long-term care until a young person is at least 18; respite breaks; parent and child placements, usually for 12 weeks; intensive support for children with complex needs; helping 16 and 17 year olds get ready to live on their own; and care for disabled children. Single people, married couples, civil partners and people in long-term relationships can all apply.

Its own criteria are that you are over 21, have a spare bedroom, live in or near Central Bedfordshire, and, if you rent, have a secure tenancy of at least two years. You need to drive and have use of a car, though for a couple one driver may be enough, and you need indefinite leave to remain or full residency in the UK.

Our Central Bedfordshire allowance page sets out the council's weekly child allowance, from £189 for a child under 5 to £278 for a young person of 16 or over, plus a carer fee of £100 a week in the first two years and £175 after that, a £1,000 welcome payment for newly approved mainstream carers, and birthday and holiday payments. The council's page does not state the year, so check current rates with the team.

Council carers get a daily duty worker, out-of-hours support, 14 nights of paid respite a year, peer mentoring, extra supervision in the first year and training built around the PACE therapeutic approach. Carers already with an agency can transfer, and the council says it will match their current fees and allowances. Talk to two or three of the services above before you choose.

Sources: Central Bedfordshire Council: types of fostering; Central Bedfordshire Council: who can foster; Central Bedfordshire Council: benefits of transferring to us; Fostering Today: Central Bedfordshire Council fostering allowances.
