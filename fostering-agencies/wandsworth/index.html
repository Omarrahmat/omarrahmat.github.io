Fostering in Wandsworth

Wandsworth Council's own service, Foster with Wandsworth, and the independent agencies that cover the borough are shown above. Fostering for the council means looking after Wandsworth's own children, supported by its fostering social workers; an agency assesses and supervises its own carers and takes children placed with it by councils. The council says its own carers are offered placements first, ahead of agency carers, and that approval takes 16 to 20 weeks on average.

It needs carers for children of every age, from babies to teenagers, and says more foster parents are needed for older children and for brothers and sisters who should stay together. Its carers can offer short-term, emergency or long-term fostering, Family Link short breaks for disabled children, respite, parent and child placements, and, with the right training or experience, remand and specialist therapeutic care.

The council's criteria are practical. You must be at least 25, with no upper age limit, and you can own or rent your home, keep pets, be retired and foster while claiming benefits. You do not need a car or any qualifications beyond the introductory training. Each foster child needs a bedroom of their own, although a baby or toddler under two can share with their main carer. You may live outside the borough, but no more than an hour from the child's school. A single carer can work part-time and flexibly; in a couple, one of you needs to be at home or working flexibly around the school day. A criminal record is looked at case by case, and the council pays for every applicant's enhanced DBS check.

Once approved, council carers have a dedicated social worker and a training programme, and the council's fostering support team runs support groups and handles payments. The council holds monthly information sessions at Wandsworth Town Hall and online; check its events page for the next date. We do not yet have a Wandsworth allowance page, so ask the council and two or three of the agencies above for their current rates in writing. The Lambeth, Merton, Sutton, Richmond upon Thames and London pages list more services nearby.

Sources: Foster with us (Wandsworth Council); Who can foster (Wandsworth Council); How fostering works (Wandsworth Council); Fostering information events (Wandsworth Council); Fostering Support Team (Wandsworth Family Information Service).
