Allowances by council

Vale of Glamorgan: foster carer allowances 2026-27.

We could not find a current 2026-27 scale of foster carer allowances on the website of Foster Wales Vale of Glamorgan, the council’s fostering service, when we last checked on 2 November 2026. Here is what the law sets as the minimum, what councils that publish their rates pay, and how to get Vale of Glamorgan’s own figures.

Ask Vale of Glamorgan directly

Councils share their rates with anyone thinking about fostering. Ask Foster Wales Vale of Glamorgan for the weekly allowance for each age band and any fee paid to the carer on top. Its fostering pages: valeofglamorgan.fosterwales.gov.wales (opens in a new tab). Phone: 01446 729600. See its profile on Fostering Today.

The legal minimum in Wales, 2026-27

Every fostering service must pay at least the national minimum allowance for the child’s costs. Most councils pay more, and many add a fee for the carer.

What councils that publish their rates pay

Across the 9 councils whose published weekly totals we checked on 27 October 2026, the typical package for one child was £375 a week for a newly approved carer and £697 with experience or a child with higher needs. Use the pay calculator to see what that means over a year, and how much of it is tax-free.

Checked

We look for Vale of Glamorgan’s published scale again regularly. If the council publishes its 2026-27 rates, or you work there and can point us to them, tell us and we will add them to this page.
