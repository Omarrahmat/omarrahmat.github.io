Allowances by council

North Yorkshire Council: foster carer allowances 2026-27.

North Yorkshire Council publishes the weekly allowances it pays its own foster carers: £200.71 to £346.12 per child for 2026-27, plus a fee for the carer. These are the council’s figures, taken from its website on 23 November 2026.

North Yorkshire pays every foster carer a weekly rate for each child in four age bands; these 2026-27 figures follow the council's decision of 3 March 2026 to raise foster carer payments by 3.4%. Once accredited, carers also receive a weekly age-related fee on top, and advanced and specialist carers are paid additional skills fees.

Fees paid to the carer

Every carer, including those not yet accredited, receives the weekly rate for the child's age; accredited carers also receive an age-related fee on top, which the council's guide shows rounded to the nearest pound. Advanced and specialist carers are paid further skills fees, which the guide lists under a 2025-26 heading, so they are not shown here.

Also published by the council

  • Birthday, Christmas and two weeks' holiday allowances, shown in the council's guide as four weeks of the child's weekly rate a year
  • Short break care paid per period of up to 24 hours, at three levels
  • Day care at £10.17 an hour from April 2026

How this compares

The national minimum allowance in England is £176 to £309 a week depending on the child’s age and the region (GOV.UK, 2026-27). The allowance covers the child’s costs; any fee is the part that is for you, and it is where councils and independent agencies differ most.

Independent agencies covering the same area publish their own weekly totals, often higher, with more expected of the carer. Use the pay calculator to see a year’s figure and the tax relief, or compare what services publish.
